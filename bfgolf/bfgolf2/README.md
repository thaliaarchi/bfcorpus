# Brainfuck Golf contest 2: Sorting

This contest was organized by Daniel B. Cristofani and [announced](https://brainfuck.sourceforge.net/current.html)
on the esoteric.sange.fi, friends-of-brainfuck, and bfgolf Yahoo mailing lists.
It was [open](https://sourceforge.net/p/brainfuck/discussion/201037/thread/9a8a1db4/)
from 2002-12-09 through 2002-12-23. Contestants first submitted the [MD5 digest](https://sourceforge.net/p/brainfuck/discussion/201037/thread/55c1e722/)
of their entry (including comments), then the [source](https://sourceforge.net/p/brainfuck/discussion/201037/thread/ed364605/),
with [live comments](https://sourceforge.net/p/brainfuck/discussion/201037/thread/1d2c5457/)
in another thread. Daniel summarized the results in results2.txt [[1](https://www.brainfuck.org/results2.txt),
[2](https://web.archive.org/web/20201221192901/http://www.hevanet.com/cristofd/brainfuck/results2.txt)].
