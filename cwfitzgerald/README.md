# Brainfuck programs by Connor Fitzgerald

Brainfuck programs written by Connor Fitzgerald in his [brainfuck-benchmarks](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark)
suite. He also wrote two Brainfuck compilers in C++, [bfcc](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/bfcc)
and [bfcc-old](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/bfcc-old).

- [bench.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/bench.b)
- [bottles.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/bottles.b)
- [deadcodetest.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/deadcodetest.b)
- [hello.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/hello.b)
- [long.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/long.b)
- [loopremove.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/loopremove.b)
- [serptri.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/serptri.b)
- [twinkle.b](https://github.com/cwfitzgerald/brainfuck-benchmark/blob/master/benches/twinkle.b)
